Havenya offers custom mobile app development for iOS and Android, enterprise software solutions, UI/UX design, API development, cloud architecture, and ongoing maintenance and support. We also develop and maintain Shopleez, our flagship e-commerce platform consisting of Customer, Admin, and Delivery applications.

Shopleez is our complete e-commerce ecosystem designed for modern businesses. It includes three applications: Shopleez Customer (for shoppers to browse and purchase products), Shopleez Admin (for business owners to manage their store, inventory, and orders), and Shopleez Delivery (for delivery personnel to manage and complete deliveries). Contact us to learn how Shopleez can power your e-commerce business.
