Our Flagship Product

Shopleez: Complete E-Commerce Platform

Shopleez is our proprietary e-commerce ecosystem, developed and published by Havenya LLC. Available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, Shopleez provides businesses with a fully integrated solution for online retail, order management, and delivery logistics.

Consumer Shopping Application

The Shopleez Customer app provides end-users with a seamless, secure shopping experience. Built with privacy-first principles, the app collects only essential data required for order processing and delivery, with full transparency about data usage as outlined in our Privacy Policy.

Intelligent Product Discovery

Advanced search algorithms with category filtering, price sorting, and personalized recommendations based on browsing history.

Secure Checkout Process

PCI-DSS compliant payment processing with encrypted transactions. Support for multiple payment methods including credit cards and digital wallets.

Real-Time Order Tracking

GPS-enabled delivery tracking with push notifications for order status updates. Users maintain full visibility from order placement to delivery.

Business Management Dashboard

The Shopleez Admin application empowers business owners with comprehensive tools to manage their e-commerce operations. Access real-time analytics, process orders, and maintain complete oversight of your business from any location.

Business Intelligence Analytics

Comprehensive dashboards with sales metrics, customer insights, revenue trends, and performance KPIs updated in real-time.

Inventory & Catalog Management

Full product lifecycle management including SKU tracking, stock alerts, bulk uploads, and automated reorder notifications.

Role-Based Access Control

Enterprise-grade permission system allowing business owners to delegate responsibilities with customizable access levels for staff members.

Delivery Driver Application

The Shopleez Delivery app provides delivery personnel with all the tools needed for efficient order fulfillment. Location services are used exclusively for delivery navigation and customer communication, with clear user consent and data handling as specified in our Privacy Policy.

Intelligent Route Planning

AI-powered route optimization considering traffic conditions, delivery windows, and multi-stop efficiency to reduce delivery times.

Digital Proof of Delivery

Capture electronic signatures and timestamped photo evidence for every successful delivery, ensuring accountability and dispute resolution.

Secure In-App Messaging

Privacy-protected communication channel between drivers and customers without exposing personal phone numbers.
